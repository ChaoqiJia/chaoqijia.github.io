About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Business Analytics & AI at RMIT University, Australia, supervised by Dr. Chao Chen and Prof. Kok-Leong Ong in the School of Accounting, Information Systems and Supply Chain.

My research focuses on machine learning and algorithm design, particularly efficient algorithms for fair and constrained clustering. I am also interested in large language models and privacy protection.

Research Interests

  • Clustering: fair clustering, constrained clustering, streaming data
  • Algorithm Design: approximation algorithms, performance guarantees
  • Machine Learning: large language models, privacy protection
